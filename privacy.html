Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Quietline, published by beastytshorts-creator ("Quietline," "we," "us," or "our"), handles information when you use the Quietline Android app.

At a glance: Quietline currently has no Quietline account system, advertising SDK, analytics service, or developer-operated backend. The app displays Instagram's mobile website, and Instagram/Meta and Google Play process data under their own policies.

1. Scope and who controls what

This policy covers the Quietline app and this policy website. It does not replace the policies that govern Instagram, Meta, Google Play, Android, or pages you choose to open outside Quietline. Quietline is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.

When you sign in to Instagram inside Quietline, the sign-in page and Instagram content are provided by Instagram in an embedded web view. Instagram/Meta controls the Instagram service and receives information that would normally be sent when you use its website. Quietline does not operate Instagram and does not receive your Instagram password through a Quietline server.

2. Information stored by Quietline on your device

Quietline stores a limited set of app data locally on your device so the app can work:

  • whether onboarding has been completed;
  • your preference for opening external links;
  • the Instagram profile path or username fallback used to open your profile;
  • the date and expiry time for the daily full-access session;
  • your acceptance of the current legal terms; and
  • Instagram web cookies, cache, and session information kept by the Android web view.

This information stays in the app's local storage unless Android, the web view, or a third-party service performs its own synchronization or backup. We do not currently transmit these local settings to a Quietline-operated server.

3. Information processed by third parties

Instagram and Meta

Instagram/Meta may process login details, account identifiers, messages, contacts, media, uploads, interactions, device and network information, cookies, and other data associated with use of Instagram. Quietline injects local interface and filtering scripts into Instagram pages to limit navigation and adapt media controls. Those scripts run on your device; they do not send Instagram content to a Quietline backend.

Review the Meta Privacy Policy and Instagram Terms of Use.

Google Play and Google Play Billing

Google processes purchase, subscription, payment, account, device, fraud-prevention, and transaction information when you install Quietline or buy access through Google Play. Quietline receives purchase-status and product-entitlement information needed to enable paid features; we do not receive your full payment-card details. Review the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms.

GitHub Pages and the issue tracker

GitHub hosts this policy site and the public support issue tracker. GitHub may process network, device, cookie, account, and usage information when you visit or submit an issue under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Quietline does not add analytics or advertising scripts to this policy site. Anything posted to the issue tracker is public, so do not submit sensitive information there.

External links

If you enable external links or choose to open another site, that site's operator may collect information under its own policy. Quietline is not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

4. How information is used

Locally stored Quietline information is used to remember preferences, maintain the restricted browsing experience, enforce the daily full-access timer, keep your legal acceptance, restore purchases, troubleshoot the app, prevent abuse, and comply with law. We do not currently sell personal data, serve targeted ads, or use app analytics.

5. Sharing

Quietline currently has no backend that shares your Instagram content or local preferences with us. Information may nevertheless be disclosed by Instagram/Meta, Google, Android, your device or backup provider, or sites you visit according to their own practices. We may disclose information we actually hold if required by law, to protect people or the app, or as part of a business transfer, subject to applicable law.

6. Retention, security, and deletion

Quietline's local settings and web-view data remain on your device until you clear the app's storage, sign out where applicable, or uninstall the app. Purchase records are retained by Google as described in Google's policies. Instagram data is retained by Meta as described in Meta's policies.

We use the platform's app sandbox and Google Play purchase mechanisms, but no system is completely secure. Protect your device and Instagram account, use a strong password, and enable appropriate account security.

For practical deletion steps, see Data & Deletion.

7. Children's privacy

Quietline is not designed for children under 13, and it is not intended for anyone who is below the minimum age required to use Instagram in their country. We do not knowingly operate a service intended to collect children's personal information. Parents or guardians with a concern may contact us.

8. International use and legal rights

Your local laws may give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive information about personal data. Because Quietline does not currently maintain a user-account backend, we may not possess data that identifies you. For Instagram or Google data, contact the applicable provider directly. We will respond to requests concerning information we control as required by applicable law.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, law, or our practices change. We will update the effective date and, where required, provide additional notice or request consent.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open a private-data-minimizing support request in the Quietline policy issue tracker. Do not post passwords, payment details, private messages, or other sensitive information in a public issue. If sensitive follow-up is required, request a private contact channel without including the sensitive material.
