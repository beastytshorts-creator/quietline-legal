Quietline Support

For help with Quietline, open a request in the public issue tracker. Quietline is independent and cannot provide Instagram account recovery, Instagram moderation, or Google account support.

Protect your information. Issues are public. Never include an Instagram or Google password, payment-card number, private message, full purchase token, government identifier, home address, or sensitive screenshot. If private follow-up is needed, ask for a private contact channel without posting the sensitive information.

Contact Quietline

Open a Quietline support request. Include your app version, Android version, device model, the screen where the problem occurs, and safe steps to reproduce it.

Purchases and subscriptions

  • Use the app's restore option while signed in to the Google Play account that made the purchase.
  • Manage or cancel an annual plan in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • For a refund, follow Trials, Cancellation & Refunds and Google's refund process.
  • If a valid purchase is not recognized, report the approximate purchase date and affected product, but do not post the complete purchase token or receipt publicly.

Instagram help

Instagram/Meta controls sign-in, account recovery, content, reporting decisions, suspensions, messages, media, and feature availability. Use the Instagram Help Center for those matters. Quietline cannot see or reset your Instagram password.

Safety reports

Use Instagram's report and block controls for content or account behavior. See the Community Guidelines. If Quietline prevents a safety control from working, report the app defect without reposting the harmful content.

Privacy and local data

Review the Privacy Policy and Data & Deletion page. A public issue should contain only non-sensitive details.

Service expectations

We aim to review support requests, but do not promise a particular response time. Instagram web changes may temporarily affect Quietline, and some account-specific Instagram features may not be available in an embedded browser.
