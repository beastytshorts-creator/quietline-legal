Trials, Cancellation & Refunds

Quietline purchases are sold, billed, and administered through Google Play. The price and terms in the Google Play purchase screen are authoritative.

Seven-day trial and annual plan

Eligible users may be offered a seven-day free trial of the annual plan. Eligibility is determined by Google Play and may be limited to users who have not used the offer before. The intended US base price after the trial is US$20 per year; Google Play displays the applicable price, currency, tax, and renewal date for your account and country.

If you do not cancel before the trial ends, Google Play will charge the displayed annual price and the subscription will renew yearly until canceled. We recommend canceling at least 24 hours before the displayed renewal time to avoid unintended renewal, while following any deadline shown by Google Play.

Lifetime purchase

The lifetime option is a one-time, non-consumable Google Play product with an intended US base price of US$50. Google Play displays the authoritative localized price. The entitlement is associated with the purchasing Google Play account and may be restored where Google Play confirms the purchase. "Lifetime" is subject to the product-availability explanation in the Terms of Use.

How to cancel

  1. Open Google Play on the account used to purchase Quietline.
  2. Open Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select Quietline and choose Cancel subscription.

Uninstalling Quietline does not cancel a subscription. After cancellation, access normally continues until the end of the paid billing period shown by Google Play.

Refund requests

Google Play processes Quietline payments and refund requests. Eligibility depends on the request timing, purchase type, circumstances, Google Play's refund rules, and applicable law. Use Google Play's refund instructions to request or check a refund.

If Google directs you to the developer or you believe the purchase is not recognized correctly, contact us through the Quietline issue tracker with the minimum information needed to investigate. Do not publish a payment card number, password, Google password, Instagram password, full purchase token, or private message. We cannot issue a refund outside the mechanisms available to us through Google Play.

Mandatory consumer rights
