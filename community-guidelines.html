Community Guidelines

Quietline does not host its own social network. It displays Instagram's service, so Instagram's Community Guidelines and Terms apply to everything you view, send, or publish there. These additional rules apply to your use of Quietline.

Use Quietline safely and lawfully

Do not use Quietline to create, access, promote, send, or facilitate:

  • child sexual abuse or exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual exploitation, or grooming;
  • credible threats, harassment, stalking, doxxing, hate, or incitement to violence;
  • fraud, impersonation, scams, coordinated manipulation, spam, or deceptive commercial activity;
  • terrorism, violent extremism, human trafficking, illegal goods or services, or instructions intended to cause serious harm;
  • malware, unauthorized account access, scraping, technical abuse, or interference with other users or services;
  • content that infringes copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, or other rights; or
  • anything unlawful or prohibited by Instagram's policies.

Reporting and blocking

Use the report, block, restrict, unfollow, or mute controls provided by Instagram on the relevant account, message, post, reel, or story. Reports about Instagram content and account behavior are reviewed by Instagram/Meta; Quietline does not have access to Instagram's moderation queue and cannot remove content from Instagram.

If a Quietline interface problem prevents you from reaching an Instagram safety control, report the app problem through the Quietline issue tracker. Describe the app behavior without reposting illegal, intimate, or highly sensitive content. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate local authority.

Enforcement

Instagram/Meta may moderate Instagram content or accounts under its rules. Separately, Quietline may restrict access to the app, decline support, or take other proportionate steps where reasonably necessary to address misuse, security, legal obligations, or repeated violations. We may preserve or disclose information we actually possess when lawfully required, but Quietline currently has no user-account backend or independent copy of Instagram content.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for your account activity and content. Respect consent, intellectual property, privacy, age restrictions, and local law. If you are unsure whether content is safe or lawful to share, do not share it.
