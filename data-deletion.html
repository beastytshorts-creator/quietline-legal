Data controls

Data & Deletion

Quietline currently does not create a separate Quietline account or store your app settings and Instagram content in a Quietline-operated backend. Different deletion steps apply to local app data, Instagram data, and Google Play records.

Delete Quietline data from your Android device

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Open Apps (the label may vary by device) and choose Quietline.
  3. Open Storage & cache, then choose Clear storage or Clear data.

This removes Quietline preferences, the local profile-path fallback, timer and legal-acceptance state, and the embedded web view's local session data on that device. You can also sign out of Instagram inside Quietline before clearing data. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data, subject to Android or device-backup behavior.

Delete or manage your Instagram data

Your Instagram account, messages, posts, reels, stories, saved items, contacts, and other Instagram data are controlled by Instagram/Meta—not Quietline. Use Instagram's account center or the Instagram account-deletion guidance to download, deactivate, or delete Instagram data. Clearing Quietline's local data does not delete your Instagram account or Instagram-hosted content.

Google Play purchases

Clearing local app data does not erase a Google Play purchase or cancel a subscription. Google controls purchase-history retention. Manage a subscription in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Review Google's privacy and account tools for deletion requests concerning Google-held data.

Request help

Because Quietline currently has no developer-operated user database, we normally have no account record to locate or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, open a minimal-data request in the Quietline policy issue tracker. Do not put sensitive data in the public issue; ask for a private follow-up channel.

We will respond to a valid request concerning information we control as required by applicable law. We cannot delete information controlled by Instagram/Meta or Google.
